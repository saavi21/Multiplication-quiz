# Multiplication-quiz
Learning multiplication tables doesn’t have to be boring — it can be fun, exciting, and full of little victories!

This website is designed especially for kids to practice, play, and master their multiplication skills at their own pace.

Here, you’ll find:

🎯 Interactive Practice – From 1×1 to 12×12, with instant feedback.
🏆 Fun Challenges – Beat the clock, earn stars, and unlock badges.
📈 Progress Tracking – See how much you’ve improved over time.
Our goal is simple: to help every child feel confident and quick with multiplication, so math becomes a friend, not a fear. Whether you’re just starting out or aiming for perfect speed, Table Champs is your safe space to learn, make mistakes, and get better every day.

Let’s turn numbers into your superpower! 🚀
